3. Space News

SpaceX launches another 27 Starlink satellites

A Falcon 9 launched 27 Starlink spacecraft from Vandenberg late Sept. 19, with the first stage landing successfully on Of Course I Still Love You. It was the booster’s 17th flight and the 81st Starlink mission of 2026, underscoring SpaceX's extraordinary operational cadence.

Source: Space.com — Sept. 20, 2026

NASA expands SpaceX's crew contract by $946 million

NASA added three more Crew Dragon missions, bringing SpaceX's commercial-crew contract to $5.92 billion and 17 astronaut flights through 2030. The extension reinforces Crew Dragon's central role in U.S. human spaceflight while NASA continues pursuing redundancy.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 18, 2026

Falcon 9's landing team receives the inaugural Neil Armstrong Space Prize

Purdue awarded its first Neil Armstrong Space Prize to the SpaceX Falcon 9 Booster Landing Team, recognizing the reusable-rocket work that transformed launch economics. Falcon 9 boosters have now accumulated hundreds of successful landings and increasingly high individual flight counts.

Source: Space.com — Sept. 17, 2026

5. Defense Industry

Anduril says Taiwan arms delays are affecting its business

Palmer Luckey said in Taipei that U.S. delays around a roughly $14 billion arms package for Taiwan are affecting Anduril's operations there. Anduril's Altius drones are already in Taiwanese service, while the company has also worked with Taiwanese partners on the Barracuda-500 missile.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 19, 2026

Lockheed moves the AIM-260 JATM toward accelerated production

Lockheed Martin publicly unveiled the long-secret AIM-260 Joint Advanced Tactical Missile and reached an agreement with the Pentagon to accelerate production. The next-generation air-to-air missile is intended for platforms including the F-22 and F-35 and reflects renewed urgency around U.S. missile inventories.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 17, 2026
