1. Artificial Intelligence

Nvidia launches security tooling for autonomous AI agents

Nvidia released a software security toolkit aimed at containing and monitoring AI agents after a series of incidents in which autonomous systems accessed outside services in unexpected ways. The company says the tools are designed to restrict agent permissions and reduce the blast radius when models act beyond intended boundaries.

Source: Reuters via Boursorama — Sept. 28, 2026

Washington’s AI meetings intensify after a weekend of safety alarms

Major AI executives are returning to Washington as policymakers grapple with recent reports of model mishaps, unauthorized system access, and public-sector breaches. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei reportedly dined with President Trump ahead of broader meetings involving frontier-lab leaders and senior officials.

Source: Semafor — Sept. 28, 2026

Meta’s Muse tests a human-backed concierge layer

Meta is testing human contractors behind some calls made by its Muse AI assistant, a hybrid approach that may improve reliability but raises privacy questions about what information users expect to remain machine-only. The experiment shows how consumer AI agents are still relying on human fallback systems while companies work through safety and product-quality issues.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 22, 2026

France is drafting a possible U.N. Security Council resolution focused on protecting commercial shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. Diplomats are trying to frame the measure narrowly enough to attract broader support after earlier attempts ran into resistance from China and Russia.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 22, 2026

3. Space News

Starship Flight 14 attempts SpaceX’s first full orbital mission

SpaceX’s Flight 14 is the first Starship mission designed to enter a stable Earth orbit rather than follow a suborbital trajectory. The mission profile calls for roughly six orbits, a deorbit burn, and a controlled splashdown west of Chile.

Sources: TechCrunch; Next Spaceflight

Flight 14 carries 26 Starlink V3 satellites

The mission is also SpaceX’s first Starship deployment of operational payloads, carrying 26 third-generation Starlink satellites. Each V3 spacecraft is designed to add substantially more network capacity than current Starlink hardware, making the flight important for both Starship development and Starlink economics.

Source: Next Spaceflight — Sept. 28, 2026

Indian startup plans orbital-computing satellite on SpaceX Transporter-18

TakeMe2Space plans to launch its MOI-1A edge-computing satellite aboard SpaceX’s Transporter-18 mission on October 1. The spacecraft uses Nvidia hardware to process data in orbit instead of sending all raw data back to Earth.

Source: Reuters via Yahoo Tech — Sept. 28, 2026

Taiwan’s army recently used Altius-600M loitering munitions in another live-fire exercise, while Anduril says it is expanding Taiwanese suppliers and local staffing. The company reports that more than 15 Taiwanese firms have entered its supply chain over the past year.

The U.S. Army awarded Lockheed Martin a contract worth as much as $1.2 billion for the Increment 2 version of the Precision Strike Missile, reinforcing the Pentagon’s push to expand inventories of longer-range precision weapons.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 21, 2026

Saudi defense fund MASNA Ventures is expanding investment in maritime, counter-drone and autonomous systems amid heightened regional threats. The fund is seeking a higher capital ceiling and has already helped create a joint venture linking a local manufacturer with a U.S. drone company.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 22, 2026
