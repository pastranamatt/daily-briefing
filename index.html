1. Artificial Intelligence

Gemini breached three real companies during a safety test

Google confirmed that Gemini inadvertently accessed systems at three real companies during a May cybersecurity evaluation after a test environment unexpectedly had internet access. The model stopped when it recognized the targets were real; the episode is another concrete warning that increasingly agentic systems can turn evaluation mistakes into real-world security incidents.

Source: Financial Times — Sept. 19, 2026

Anthropic and Accenture commit $2 billion to independent model evaluation

Anthropic and Accenture announced at least $2 billion over five years for frontier-model evaluations, red-teaming and alignment assessments. The effort reflects a broader shift toward outside testing as labs confront reports of unexpected autonomous behavior and pressure for more credible safety oversight.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 18, 2026

U.S.-China AI safety cooperation remains stuck behind strategic rivalry

Washington and Beijing both acknowledge serious AI risks, but efforts to create shared safety norms are colliding with fears that restraint could hand the other side an advantage. The result is a governance problem in which strategic competition keeps narrowing the space for meaningful cooperation.

Source: Associated Press — Sept. 17, 2026

2. Foreign Affairs

U.S., Denmark and Greenland reach a new Arctic security agreement

The United States, Denmark and Greenland finalized an agreement expanding long-term U.S. military access and restricting sensitive investment by non-NATO countries. The arrangement strengthens Washington's Arctic posture while easing, at least for now, the political dispute surrounding President Trump's earlier interest in acquiring Greenland.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 18, 2026

Russia includes occupied Ukrainian regions in parliamentary voting

Russia is holding parliamentary voting in Ukrainian territories it claims to have annexed, including parts of Donetsk, Luhansk, Kherson and Zaporizhzhia. Ukraine and the EU reject the voting as illegal, while reports of coercion underscore how the election is also being used to consolidate Russian political control over occupied areas.

Source: Associated Press — Sept. 19, 2026

Iran executes a man convicted of spying for Israel

Iran executed Hossein Pedaran after convicting him of providing information on missile sites to Israel's Mossad. The case reflects the continued security crackdown and intelligence contest between Iran and Israel following this year's direct conflict.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 19, 2026

3. Space News

NASA gives SpaceX three more crew missions in a $946 million expansion

NASA added three Crew Dragon missions to SpaceX's Commercial Crew contract, lifting the agreement's value to $5.92 billion and extending SpaceX flights through 2030. The award reinforces Crew Dragon's position as NASA's primary operational U.S. crew transport while Boeing works to stabilize Starliner.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 18, 2026

NASA is discussing 10 or more future Starliner missions with Boeing

Despite Starliner's troubled 2024 crewed test, NASA is discussing a larger future mission slate with Boeing. Maintaining a viable second provider would reduce NASA's dependence on SpaceX and preserve redundancy for human access to low Earth orbit.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 18, 2026

SpaceX has flown two classified Space Force missions in under a week

A Falcon 9 launched the classified USSF-259 mission from Vandenberg on Sept. 16, six days after another classified Space Force launch. It was SpaceX's 110th launch of 2026, illustrating both Falcon 9's extraordinary cadence and SpaceX's growing role in national-security space.

Source: Space.com — Sept. 17, 2026

Temporal's Series E was led by Lightspeed with Wellington, Goldman Sachs Alternatives and Tiger Global as co-leads; returning investors include a16z and Sequoia. The durable-execution company says annualized revenue run rate has surpassed $250 million, up more than 200% year over year, as AI agents create demand for reliable long-running workflows.

Source: Temporal — Sept. 14, 2026

Impulse Space adds $308 million, taking its Series D to $808 million

The in-space transportation company founded by former SpaceX propulsion chief Tom Mueller expanded its Series D to $808 million as its government and commercial backlog grows. The capital will support spacecraft development, manufacturing and hiring, with the company valued at about $5.4 billion in reporting on the extension.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 16, 2026

Ridgeline raised a $250 million Series E at a $1.425 billion valuation. The company is trying to replace legacy asset-management systems with a unified cloud platform spanning trading, accounting, compliance and reporting, with AI operating on a shared data model.

5. Defense Industry

Anduril says delayed Taiwan arms approvals are affecting its business

Palmer Luckey said in Taipei that delays around a roughly $14 billion U.S. arms package for Taiwan are affecting Anduril's operations there. The company's Altius drones are already used by Taiwan, and Anduril has co-developed the Barracuda-500 missile with Taiwanese partners, making the stalled approvals commercially and strategically significant.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 19, 2026

GM is already delivering parts for Patriot interceptors

GM Defense delivered its first PAC-3 MSE housing components to Lockheed Martin just 22 days after the manufacturing agreement was signed. The unusually fast handoff is a tangible example of the Pentagon and primes pulling commercial industrial capacity into the effort to expand depleted missile inventories.

Source: Reuters — Sept. 17, 2026

Anduril expands Fury's strike role with air-to-ground weapons testing

Anduril has conducted ground fit-checks of guided bombs, rocket pods and other air-to-ground weapons on its YFQ-44A Fury autonomous aircraft. The internally funded integration work points toward a broader mission set for the Air Force's Collaborative Combat Aircraft beyond its original air-to-air emphasis.

Source: The Defense News — Sept. 16, 2026
